About Grace Awaits

Graduate of D. Law | D. Management Assistant | Cum Laude - Theology | Minister at Heart

I am Sharifa Dudley, founder of Grace Awaits. Grace Awaits is a professional business support platform offering practical, creative, and administrative solutions to individuals, entrepreneurs, churches, and businesses. Our services range from career support, graphic design, and web development to administrative and paralegal support, company and CIPC services, document preparation, and event planning.

Rooted in love and faithfulness, our goal is to provide reliable, professional, and personalised support that helps our clients turn ideas into action.
